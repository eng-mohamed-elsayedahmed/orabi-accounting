Income statement for social housing applications

The request we receive most during application seasons. Here is the full document list — bring it in and the certificate is issued stamped and certified by the office.

CERTIFIED AND STAMPED
  • Copy of the national ID for the applicant and spouse
  • Marriage certificate and birth certificates of children
  • Proof of income — the figure stated must be net monthly income
  • If married: the spouse salary breakdown, or a declaration that the spouse does not work
  • A recent utility bill for the current residence: electricity, gas, or water
  • Documents for additional income sources: second job, agricultural land, property, exceptional pension, shares, bonds, deposits, or savings accounts
  • For professionals and commercial or craft activities: a certificate from a chartered accountant stating net annual or monthly income and the profession, plus the commercial register or tax card
  • For widows and pensioners: a certified pension printout from social insurance — and for divorcees, the alimony document
  • Family registration record for married, divorced, or widowed applicants
  • Social insurance printout

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Direct answers, no detours

When is the tax return deadline?

31 March for a natural person and 30 April for a legal entity, covering the previous financial year. Filing is electronic and mandatory — paper returns are not accepted under any circumstances.

How high do late-filing penalties go?

Up to 60 days late: EGP 3,000 to 50,000. More than 60 days late: EGP 50,000 to 2,000,000. Delay costs far more than the service itself.

My activity is not registered for tax. What now?

As the state widens its survey of the informal economy, the activity will be registered sooner or later. Registering by choice is simpler and cheaper, and it prevents the matter being treated as tax evasion. The procedure is straightforward and we handle it at the office.

I need an income certificate for social housing. What do I bring?

The full list is in the individuals section above. In short: national ID, marriage and birth certificates, proof of income, a recent utility bill, documents for any additional income, and a social insurance printout. The certificate is issued stamped and certified by the office.

What does company formation actually require?

The incorporation deed, tax card, commercial register, social insurance file, and VAT registration certificate. If your activity involves import or export, we obtain that card as well.

Do you work with clients outside Cairo?

Yes. Most tax procedures are now electronic, so communication and follow-up run over WhatsApp and email. Visits to the office are limited to steps that genuinely require a signature or original documents.
